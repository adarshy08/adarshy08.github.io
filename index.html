Low-Cost Lightweight Milk Chilling Can

A can for small-scale dairy farmers and door-to-door milkmen who carry milk on cycles or two-wheelers, where electricity and bulk coolers are not available. Milk spoils within hours in hot weather, and this design keeps it cold without any power.

No powerpassive cooling
PCM pouchesphase change material
Lightfits a cycle or bike mount
  • Problem: milk delivered in summer heat loses quality before it reaches customers.
  • Approach: sealed phase change material (PCM) cartridges inside a lightweight can; the PCM absorbs heat and holds milk temperature low during the delivery round.
  • Status: design developed; invention disclosure form being prepared at LPU. Guide: Dr. Himanshu Singh.
  • Next: prototype, temperature-vs-time testing, cost comparison.
